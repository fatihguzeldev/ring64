# reset and mode transitions

Status: implementation-ready specification; implementation not started. Source audit baseline: `4df41a0814beaceb0681dc013f5eac094cdc6660`. Fourth of five, after [numeric controls](../numeric-execution-controls/spec.md) and [legacy paging](../legacy-paging-and-permissions/spec.md).

## complete objective and inherited boundaries

On one physical-backed CPU, reset to a selected real-address image, execute guest initialization, enter protected/non-PAE/PAE/IA32e execution through actual control writes and far transfers, perform work and return to real execution without replacing RAM. Preserve raw state, loaded-cache lifetimes, exact fault/completion boundaries, pending-source meaning and finite generated ownership across that story. Imported destination snapshots alone do not prove transitions.

Extend only the new `memoryModel: 'physical'` plane. Keep private-linear execution and the `ia32e-paged` convenience model's IA32e-only constructor/control frontier. A physical backing flag or an optional paging bank cannot distinguish these capabilities by itself. No platform devices, BIOS/OS boot target, A20 switching, INIT/SIPI, SMM, task switching, TLB/PCID/global retention, CPUID or clock provider is added. A20 is enabled. A selected power-on-style projection is distinct from constructor initialization, FINIT/FNINIT and hardware warm reset.

Keep the physical plane and its control bank present even at PG0. `paging.is_some()` must not mean paging is enabled or that family transitions are authorized. The baseline uses that test in decoder/discovery, memory adapters, emitter and observation; split backing-plane identity, control availability and active family at their owning boundary, rather than setting the bank to None or adding walkers in callers.

## physical PG0 access and fault provenance

PG0 uses the same physical backing/alias/permission/prepared-store authority as paged families, with zero walk/A/D/quartet work. The reached legacy linear start is `(base+offset) mod2^32`, after inherited segment admission; the complete-span2^32 private refusal remains. Ordinary PG0 accesses reach only the32-bit physical range. The backing store may still contain sparse52-bit addresses for host administration/later paged access; this does not make them directly reachable with PG0 operands.

Fetch, explicit operands, descriptor/TSS/event/IRET traffic, numeric images and atomic/RMW preparation all use identity translation through that authority. Prepared PG0 spans contain no fake leaf/dirty-entry metadata. Read-only or missing physical backing is an engine `physical-memory-fault` with phase `final-access`, a legitimate formed linear `address` equal to `physicalAddress`, actual access/width/failing address and inherited segment/system provenance. There are no fabricated page-table level/entry fields, #PF, CR2 update or guest page-table accesses.

Literal unbacked-reset-vector result: instructionPc=`0xfff0`, address/physicalAddress/failingPhysicalAddress=`0xfffffff0`, width1, access/physicalAccess execute, phase final-access, cause unmapped, segmentcs; CR2=0 and no vector14 source. Backing physical0xffff0 instead must not satisfy that fetch. A valid identity-mapped page-table-looking region has no special effects in PG0.

## full snapshot, constructor and bank ownership

Physical construction still requires explicit memory/entry/context/paging and captures one coherent native candidate **before** Core publication. Validate active family, loaded caches, conditional legacy-PAE quartet and optional fast-system bank together; do not initialize default Long64 Core and then install a supposedly atomic legacy context afterward. PG0/IA32e reject the `pdpte` property even if undefined; active legacy PAE requires all four captured raw entries. Host imports do not load PDPT RAM. Preserve the spec3 raw CR2/CR3 image and effective-root distinctions.

Keep the public `ExecutionContextSnapshot` shape. Internally, physical-plane-aware **full** capture/readback permits retained raw unsigned64 segment and system-table/cache bases across all active families, including noncanonical FS/GS and system roots, and retained unusable/null SS. A legal PG/LMA activation preserves these raw facts rather than inventing a new activation #GP; the resulting physical full snapshot must remain readable and restorable. Existing legacy shorthand inputs and strict private-linear/`ia32e-paged` shapes remain unchanged. Virtual8086 also keeps its fixed six usable selector-derived64KiB/D0 cache constraints; the retained-wide/unusable relaxation does not weaken that separate profile. Apply this distinction in CPU initial capture, native mapped candidate parsing, `setContext`, `readContext`/JSON parsing and atomic restore; relaxing only the constructor leaves a guest-generated snapshot unreadable.

For physical full snapshots permit usable read-write CS at CPL0 in protected/compatibility, with VM0 and compatibility CS.L0. This is the reachable reset/real-cache→PE1→LMA1 transitional image. It needs neither a hidden provenance flag nor a descriptor reread. CS type still must be executable for ordinary CPL1–3 protected/compatibility inputs; actual far descriptor loads retain their own code-type validation. The CS role admits fetch from the retained transitional cache; its loaded type continues to govern ordinary CS data access. D/limit/type/attributes remain explicit loaded facts.

Outside IA32e, effective segment/system addresses use the inherited32-bit projection; stored wide FS/GS and roots are not clipped. An unusable SS can survive a mode change/checkpoint and fault on a later stack access rather than invalidating a NOP-only restore. Actual IA32e address use keeps its own canonical/address rules; WRMSR, descriptor loads and other architectural writers retain their instruction-specific canonical checks. Raw checkpoint transport does not perform a speculative operand access. Select preservation of raw GDTR/IDTR/LDTR/TR upper bits across family changes where the admitted profile needs a deterministic checkpoint; do not claim hardware parity for unspecified high system-root behavior. FS/GS and loaded segment retention have their manual-backed lifetime. Preserve raw R8–15/XMM8–15 through legacy excursions as an explicit deterministic choice where Intel declares their values undefined after leaving IA32e. New guest register writes retain their instruction-specific merge/zero rules.

This spec supersedes spec3's physical `fastSystem` rejection: physical CPUs accept an optional complete bank, omission initializes zero and explicit undefined rejects, with existing normalized fields/canonical restrictions and detached idle read/set methods in PG0/legacy/IA32e. Family changes preserve it and reset zeros it. EFER stays in the paging bank; live FS/GS bases remain loaded-context aliases, not copies in the fast bank. Idle setters individually require consistency with live other authorities and preserve historical events under their own cancellation rules. Complete cross-family host restore uses an atomic new CPU constructor; no aggregate mutable checkpoint API is required.

## synchronous reset and capture barrier

Add `reset(): void` on idle physical CPUs, with no options. It performs no guest memory read/write, page walk, instruction retirement or event entry and need not find backed reset-vector bytes. Preserve memory bytes, aliases, backing incarnations, mappings/permissions, limits and host CPU identity; do not recreate Image or free/reallocate RAM. Preserve embedding providers when later present; reset is not a device reset.

Busy runs and browser-compilation waits, wrong model, failed/disposed lifecycle reject **without** canceling work, advancing reset identity or partially clearing state. Reset does not reopen a failed/disposed or exhausted-ID CPU. On success publish the fixed architectural reset candidate, clear transient execution/diagnostic work, retire all native pending/installed/dormant source owners and assumptions, then clear host translation/index/LRU references. Release is coordinated/idempotent; no reachable intermediate state is exposed. Do not reset the opaque owner-ID allocator: old ownerA must never become ownerB after reset, even when context_version is already u64MAX.

Accepted reset is also a capture barrier. If an outer capture for that same existing CPU calls reset from a user getter, that old outer capture must reject afterward without undoing reset. Existing `requireIdle()` alone cannot detect this because reset ends idle. Use a private fresh reset token or non-saturating reset generation checked by **every** capture-based public operation alongside its existing lifecycle/run-generation rule: mappings/backing capture, scalar/context, XMM/MXCSR/x87, debug, paging and fast-system imports, run option capture and byte-input capture where it can call user code. Audit actual consumers rather than only the newest setters.

The token is per CPU: a new constructor or another CPU's capture is not invalidated because its getter resets a different existing CPU. This barrier is specific to reset. It does not broaden earlier policies for arbitrary nested idle setters or force a new public global epoch. Do not reset/wrap a run-generation counter or make reset depend on available run-generation increments; accepted reset still works at native epochMAX and near/exhausted host run-generation capacity, while a later run retains its existing exhaustion rule. Rejected reset changes neither token nor counters.

## privileged consumers exposed by reset

CR/MSR/CLTS/INVLPG decoding must depend on physical control availability and actual instruction legality, not PG1 or the current IA32e-only `prefixes.paged` shortcut. Admit selected EFER and existing fast-bank/FS-GS RDMSR/WRMSR at physical CPL0 in real/protected/compatibility/Long64, retaining existing index/value/canonical/width rules. In particular RDMSR publishes zero-extended EAX/EDX under the existing MSR contract, while ordinary legacy MOV-from-CR uses its32-bit GPR merge rule. Unknown indices retain Unsupported, and privilege checks precede selected values. This enables reset initialization without fabricating a linear CPU control image. Physical PG0 INVLPG is admitted at real/protected CPL0 with its existing address-only semantics and destructive fence, zero target read/walk/A-D; register operand/LOCK select #UD and VM86/CPL denial #GP.

Do not broaden the fast instruction profile accidentally: SYSCALL/SYSRET/SWAPGS retain their selected Long64/IA32e legality; SYSENTER/SYSEXIT keep the predecessor's IA32e Long64/compatibility semantics. Legacy SYSENTER/SYSEXIT and other deferred fast facilities remain an explicit private frontier. Merely storing a bank while temporarily in PG0 does not claim those instructions execute there. FS/GS setters, guest segment reloads, WRMSR and SWAPGS retain one live base authority.

Dynamic DE also needs consumers. With DE0, DR4 aliasesDR6 and DR5 aliasesDR7; normalize/write/check the aliased register under the existing debug rules. With DE1, those forms select #UD before CPL/GD; BD/GD state is unchanged by that illegal form. Keep the selected predecessor legality → privilege → GD → transfer order, RF saved/live distinctions and frozen sources. With DE0 select inactive/nonmatching behavior for DR7 R/W=10, whose hardware meaning is undefined there; preserve its readback bits and do not turn it into a memory watchpoint. This adds no I/O-watchpoint coverage. DE changes revoke specialized legality assumptions; a cold probe and retained native entry must agree.

NE resets0 and becomes writable. After spec2 enable admission, a pending x87 condition at any existing waiting/pending-#MF consumer with NE0 returns existing `unsupported` reason3 at that PC, retires0, preserves live RF/pending numeric state, performs no operand and creates no processor event, IRQ13, frame or `x87Exception` falsely naming vector16. This includes x87/WAIT, applicable legacy images, MMX/EMMS/MOVNTQ/MASKMOVQ and pending-checking bridges; CVTPI2PD memory and pure SSE/FX exemptions remain. No new external error controller is added.

An instruction newly posting an unmasked x87 condition keeps its normal producer publication/retirement; the external frontier is at the next waiting consumer, not a rollback of the producer. FNCLEX/FNINIT can repair without waiting, or setting NE1 exposes the existing #MF/report/delivery route with original producer metadata. #NM/admission #UD precedes the NE decision; WAIT's MP/TS gate is still special. SIMD #XM/numeric #UD ignores NE. A tiny typed unsupported-cause record may distinguish this existing reason if needed by the transport, but no new event type or generic provider framework is required.

## completion, resources and generated lifetime

Fetch and execute a control writer under its source family. After commit, successor fetch and operands use the destination family and raw loaded facts. Do not restart the writer through a new root, replay prepared accesses or undo a completed mode change because successor fetch/translation/compile refuses. Preserve its retirement, TF/RF/SS history and eligible IRQ/NMI arbitration before post-work budget/stop/target gates. Event selection at a completed writer uses the committed destination envelope and existing event budget. Successor fetch itself is later than post-work instruction budget/stop gates: a last-budget writer returns with committed state, and the next positive-budget resume may then fetch/fault. A required successor fault outranks a later compilation quota, not an exhausted instruction budget.

Family/root/permission changes retain destructive fences; numeric-only changes retain spec2 eligibility-aware admission. Include family, DE/numeric facts, loaded context and captured roots in the actual owner proof; revoking a boolean without comparing relevant captured facts is insufficient. Reset clears every pending/installed/dormant proof and source watcher while preserving allocator identity. Ordinary family transitions do not reset numeric/debug/entry banks, NMI blocking or frozen selected sources. Reset intentionally clears them all.

Cold `core_probe` keeps required-fetch/decode and spec2 enable-admission semantics. It must not execute an uncompiled control writer, load a PDPT or evaluate a transition to evade compilation limits. Exhausted cold compilation capacity may return resource-limit before that writer, with no transition. A warm reached writer still produces exact architectural/private failure; a completed writer followed by fetch #PF/private refusal wins over the later compilation quota. This avoids a second speculative control interpreter.
